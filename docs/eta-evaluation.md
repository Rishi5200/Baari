# ETA evaluation: Default vs Rolling

Generated by `com.baari.eta.Backtest` from `tokens.csv`. Do not edit by hand; rerun the backtest.

## Setup

- Data: simulated clinic days (`baari-simulator`, `sim/demo-clinic.json`), 60 days: 2026-09-21 to 2026-11-19.
- Scored: the newest 14 days (2026-11-06 to 2026-11-19). Rolling on a day only sees consultations from earlier days (time-based, never a random split).
- Predictions are logged at the production triggers: JOIN and the first time a patient is within 10, 5, 3 and 1 places. Patients who never reached a consultation (no-shows) are not scored.
- The wait is the raw projector output (no display smoothing). Default assumes 6 min per consultation; both use a 30 s gap between patients and no no-show allowance, as production does today.
- Error = predicted minus actual wait, in minutes. Bias above 0 means the estimate was too long. Coverage = share of actual waits inside the shown p20 to p80 range (the range is built to cover about 60%).

## Reading

Rolling's mean error is 7.4 min against 8.6 min for Default (13% lower). The shown range holds the actual wait 45% of the time for Rolling and 44% for Default.

The range is meant to hold about 60% of actual waits, so both are too narrow, and both lean short (negative bias, worst at JOIN). The projector does not yet model late session starts, a doctor slowing down through a session or no-shows, and it has no calibration factor (PLAN 8.5). These are the first things to fix before an ML model is compared with Rolling.
