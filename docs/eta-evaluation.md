# ETA evaluation: Default vs Rolling vs calibrated Rolling

Generated by `com.baari.eta.Backtest` from `tokens.csv`. Do not edit by hand; rerun the backtest.

## Setup

- Data: simulated clinic days (`baari-simulator`, `sim/demo-clinic.json`), 60 days: 2026-09-22 to 2026-11-20.
- Scored: the newest 14 days (2026-11-07 to 2026-11-20). Rolling on a day only sees consultations from earlier days (time-based, never a random split).
- Predictions are logged at the production triggers: JOIN and the first time a patient is within 10, 5, 3 and 1 places. Patients who never reached a consultation (no-shows) are not scored.
- The wait is the raw projector output (no display smoothing). Default assumes 6 min per consultation; all variants use a 30 s gap between patients and no no-show allowance.
- ROLLING+CALIBRATION is what production runs: Rolling once the clinic has enough history, with the range widened by the nightly calibration factor (PLAN 8.5: coverage of the last 14 days, one step of 0.1 per night toward 60 to 70%, kept within 1.0 to 2.5). It was replayed over every day before the test window as well; the factor was 2.1 on the first scored day and 1.2 after the last.
- Error = predicted minus actual wait, in minutes. Bias above 0 means the estimate was too long. Coverage = share of actual waits inside the shown range (the target is 60 to 70%).

## Reading

Rolling's mean error is 7.4 min against 8.5 min for Default (13% lower). Without calibration the shown range holds the actual wait 45% of the time (Rolling) and 44% (Default), short of the 60% target; with calibration it holds it 66% of the time. Calibration only widens the range; it does not move the estimate, so the mean error stays the same.

All variants lean short (negative bias, worst at JOIN). Two causes are known and not modelled: doctors start sessions late, and patients who join before the session are projected as if the doctor were already there (the app stores no session timetable yet). Allowing for no-shows was tried and made the error worse here, because a no-show still costs the doctor a few minutes of waiting.
